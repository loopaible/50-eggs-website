The Concept

Comfort Food, Done Right

Spring Chicken is Southern cooking with real technique, reimagined for a quick lunch or an easy dinner — nothing fussy, nothing cut short.

Everything starts from scratch: chicken brined and hand-breaded to order, biscuits baked fresh through the day, sauces and slaws made in-house.

The result is food that feels familiar and generous, whether you’re grabbing a sandwich to go or feeding the whole table.

Explore the Menu

The Lineage

Born From Yardbird

Spring Chicken comes straight out of Yardbird Southern Table & Bar — 50 Eggs’ award-winning ode to Southern cooking, and the restaurant that put founder John Kunkel’s fried chicken on the map.

The idea was simple: take the recipes, technique and hospitality that made Yardbird a destination, and make them fast, casual and everyday.

Same buttermilk brine, same 27-hour process, same scratch biscuits — now served in minutes, at a counter, for a fraction of the fuss.
