A Legacy Of Concepts

Our Previous Successes

Before Wakuda, Chica, Spring Chicken and Bonanno Brothers, these were the restaurants that built our reputation — and the standard every concept since has had to live up to.

Yardbird Table & Bar

Founder John Kunkel’s original Southern kitchen and the restaurant that put 50 Eggs on the national map — a James Beard Award semifinalist nod and a spot on Bon Appétit’s 50 Best New Restaurants list. Its fried chicken and hospitality standard live on today in Spring Chicken.

Swine Southern Table & Bar

A Coral Gables sibling to Yardbird, built around a whole-animal butchery program and scratch Southern cooking — another early proof point for the standard 50 Eggs still holds every concept to.
