Modern Japanese Cuisine

Wakuda

The Concept

Inspired by the energy of Tokyo’s Shinjuku ward, Wakuda captures the tension between traditional and modern Japan through its cuisine, design and art.

Chef Tetsuya Wakuda built his reputation on precision, purity and respect for the ingredient, marrying French technique with Japanese philosophy across acclaimed restaurants from Sydney to Singapore.

“Iron sharpens iron” is foundational to his work — a belief in collaboration with admirable contemporaries that shapes every dish that leaves the kitchen.

Wakuda is Chef Tetsuya’s first restaurant in the United States, bringing his ingredient-driven philosophy to Las Vegas alongside his original Singapore location.

About 50 Eggs

Who We Are

50 Eggs Hospitality Group started in 2001 when founder John Kunkel opened a simple sandwich shop on Alton Road in Miami Beach.

Since then, we’ve grown into a global brand operating four restaurant concepts, from Miami to Las Vegas, Los Angeles and Singapore.

But we’re more than concepts and locations.

We’re a group of people who care deeply about excellence and how that excellence affects those around us.
