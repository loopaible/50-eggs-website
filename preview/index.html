What we do best

Restaurant concepts

Four distinct concepts — Wakuda, Chica, Spring Chicken and Bonano Pizza — each built around its own chef, menu and point of view.

Corporate dining

Private dinners, executive gatherings and all-day meeting spaces at Wakuda and Chica in The Venetian, Las Vegas, with menus designed around your group.

Private events & buyouts

Hospitality consulting
