Modern Japanese · The Venetian, Las Vegas

Wakuda

Two-Michelin-starred Chef Tetsuya Wakuda’s first U.S. restaurant — a modern Japanese dining room inspired by Tokyo’s Shinjuku ward, with an artisan-crafted Kumiko ceiling, a terrace with views of Las Vegas Boulevard and a hidden bar.

Choose from an intimate private dining room, a private eight-seat omakase experience, the secret bar or the full restaurant. Breakfast events feature inventive morning dishes and American favorites with refined Japanese touches.

  • Main Dining Room110 seated · 150 standing
  • The TerraceUp to 150 guests
  • Private Dining Room12–14 seated
  • Omakase Room8 seats
  • Secret BarUp to 30 guests
  • Full BuyoutUp to 400 guests

Latin American · The Venetian, Las Vegas

Chica

A lively celebration of Latin American flavors and culture — hand-painted murals, a wraparound bar with color-changing LED lighting and projection, and a garden-inspired room that brings Miami energy to the Strip.

Host in the semi-private lounge or the wrought-iron library, book one of two private dining rooms with AV, or take the full restaurant. A hospitality suite offers all-day catering and conference-style setups, and breakfast events include build-your-own avocado toast, breakfast tacos and a pancake and donut station.

  • Main Dining RoomUp to 80 seated
  • The BarUp to 100 guests
  • The LoungeUp to 35 guests
  • The Library30–40 guests
  • Private Dining Rooms (2)12–30 guests · AV
  • Full BuyoutUp to 325 guests

3355 S Las Vegas Blvd, Suite 106, Las Vegas, NV · (702) 805-8472
