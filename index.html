About 50 Eggs

Who We Are

50 Eggs Hospitality Group started in 2001 when founder John Kunkel opened a simple sandwich shop on Alton Road in Miami Beach.

Since then, we’ve grown into a global brand operating four restaurant concepts, from Miami to Las Vegas, Los Angeles and Singapore.

But we’re more than concepts and locations.

We’re a group of people who care deeply about excellence and how that excellence affects those around us.

Our Story

Multi-Concept Hospitality Group

Restaurateurs, Creatives & Hospitality Visionaries

Since 2001, 50 Eggs Hospitality Group has been built by chefs, designers and operators who believe a great restaurant is as much about people as it is about the plate.

  • Restaurant Concepts↓

    From Wakuda’s modern Japanese cuisine to Chica’s Latin flavors and Spring Chicken’s Southern comfort food, each concept is built with its own identity, menu and point of view.

  • Leadership↓

    Founder John Kunkel opened his first restaurant in Miami Beach in 2011 and has since built 50 Eggs into a hospitality group recognized by the James Beard Foundation, Forbes and Condé Nast Traveler.

  • Hospitality & Design↓

    Every space we open is designed in-house, from the dining room to the menu, so the food, the room and the service all tell the same story.
