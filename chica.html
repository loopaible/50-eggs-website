Chica

The Concept

Born from a celebrated celebrity-chef partnership, Chica is a vibrant celebration of pan-Latin cuisine — the bold flavors of Venezuela, Peru, Brazil, Mexico and Argentina brought to one lively table.

Established

2014

The Concept

Latin Celebration

Chica is more than a restaurant—it’s an invitation to gather. Rooted in a renowned celebrity-chef collaboration, the concept translates the warmth and energy of Latin America into a modern dining experience.

The menu travels across the region, from Venezuelan arepas and Peruvian ceviche to wood-fired Argentine cuts, each dish built on family recipes and bold, layered flavor.

With locations in Miami and Las Vegas, Chica pairs that food with rhythm, color and genuine hospitality — a place designed for long tables and longer nights.

Explore Chica

The Kitchen

Celebrity-Chef Roots

Chica was shaped by a celebrated celebrity chef whose career carried Latin cooking onto national stages — television, cookbooks and acclaimed restaurants alike.

That pedigree lives on in the kitchen’s standards: scratch cooking, house-made sauces and salsas, and a respect for the regional traditions behind every plate.

Today a dedicated culinary team carries the concept forward, keeping the food bold, generous and true to its pan-Latin origins.
