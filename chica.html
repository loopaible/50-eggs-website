The Concept

Latin Celebration

Wakuda is more than a restaurant—it’s an expression of culinary philosophy refined across decades and continents. Every element, from the ingredient selection to the plating technique, reflects a commitment to precision and purity.

The menu evolves with the seasons, showcasing the finest ingredients from Japan and beyond, prepared with meticulous attention to their inherent qualities.

Whether dining at the Las Vegas location in The Venetian or experiencing the original Singapore restaurant, guests discover an intersection of tradition and innovation where every dish tells a story.

Meet the Chef

Chef Lorena Garcia

Renowned for his mastery of Japanese cuisine and respect for the ingredient, Chef Lorena Garcia has built an international reputation across acclaimed restaurants from Sydney to Singapore.

His philosophy blends French technique with Japanese precision, creating dishes that honor tradition while pushing culinary boundaries. Each plate reflects years of dedication to understanding ingredients and their potential.

At Wakuda, Chef Tetsuya brings his ingredient-driven approach and collaborative spirit to Las Vegas, bringing his Singapore concept’s success to new audiences and expanding his culinary vision globally.
