Champions Sport Kitchen

Where live sports meets chef-driven dining. A new 50 Eggs concept built for the modern fan.

The Concept

More Than a Sports Bar

Champions Sport Kitchen is what happens when a hospitality group that builds destination restaurants turns its attention to the sports bar. The result: a chef-driven kitchen, premium cocktails, wall-to-wall screens, and an atmosphere that makes every game night an event.

This isn’t a sports bar with better food. It’s a restaurant with the energy of live sports — designed for fans who want both.
