Our Story

From One Idea to a Hospitality Company

50 Eggs Hospitality Group was born in Miami in 2001, when founder John Kunkel opened Lime Fresh Mexican Grill with a simple belief: a restaurant should be more than a place to eat. It should be a brand.

That idea grew into Yardbird Table & Bar, a James Beard semifinalist that became one of the most talked-about openings in Miami Beach. Then Swine, Khong River House, Chica and Wakuda — each one a new concept, built from zero, designed to become a destination.

Today, 50 Eggs is a full-service hospitality development and management company. We create original restaurant brands, operate them at the highest level, and consult for owners, developers and venues who want food & beverage that actually works.

Leadership

John Kunkel

Founder & CEO

John Kunkel founded 50 Eggs Hospitality Group in 2001 with the opening of Lime Fresh Mexican Grill, which grew into a regional brand before being acquired by Ruby Tuesday.

He went on to create Yardbird Table & Bar, named a James Beard Foundation Best New Restaurant semifinalist and one of Bon Appétit’s 50 Best New Restaurants. That success led to Swine, Khong River House, Chica and Wakuda — each concept reflecting his belief that great restaurants are built on original ideas, not formulas.

John’s approach is hands-on. He develops every concept from the ground up, stays close to operations, and treats each brand as a long-term investment in a city’s dining culture.

Long-Term Thinking
