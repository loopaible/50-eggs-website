People first.
Plates second.

The chefs, designers and crews behind 50 Eggs Hospitality Group.

Founded in 2001 by John Kunkel, 50 Eggs set out to become the best restaurant group in the business.

It started with a simple sandwich shop on Alton Road in Miami Beach. Full of ambition and a belief that hospitality was about people first, Kunkel built a name for hand-crafted, chef-driven food served without pretense.

That philosophy grew into Yardbird Table & Bar, the restaurant that put 50 Eggs on the national map with a James Beard Award semifinalist nod and a spot on Bon Appétit’s 50 Best New Restaurants list.

Today, 50 Eggs operates four distinct concepts — Wakuda, Chica, Spring Chicken and Bonanno Brothers — spanning Miami, Las Vegas, Los Angeles and Singapore, each built by chefs and creatives who bring their own point of view to the table.

Great hospitality starts with great people.

Meet Our Concepts

A Group Built On Craft

We partner with chefs who define their category — from Tetsuya Wakuda’s two-Michelin-starred pedigree to the celebrity-chef roots behind Chica’s celebration of Latin flavors — and give them the room to build something singular.

Behind every concept is a team of leaders, from our restaurant operators to Pedro Martinez, who leads our people and culture, and Greg Thomas, who helps steer the business forward.
