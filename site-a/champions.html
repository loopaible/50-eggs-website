Champions Sport Kitchen

Where live sports meets chef-driven dining. A new 50 Eggs concept built for the modern fan.

The Concept

More Than a Sports Bar

Champions Sport Kitchen is what happens when a hospitality group that builds destination restaurants turns its attention to the sports bar. The result is something entirely new: a chef-driven kitchen, premium cocktails, wall-to-wall screens, and an atmosphere that makes every game night an event.

This isn’t a sports bar with better food. It’s a restaurant with the energy of live sports — designed for fans who want both.

What Makes It Different

Three Pillars

🍴

Chef-Driven Kitchen

A menu built by the same team behind Wakuda and Chica. Elevated comfort food — smoked brisket sliders, wagyu burgers, hand-cut fries — made to share and designed for game day.

🍷

Premium Bar

Craft cocktails, local beers on tap and a curated spirits selection. The drink program is designed to match the energy — fast, fun and high quality.

🏅

Big-Screen Experience

State-of-the-art AV built for every seat in the house. No bad views, no dead zones. The screens are part of the design, not an afterthought.
