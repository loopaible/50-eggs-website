What We Do

Beyond the Table

We don’t just build our own restaurants. We bring two decades of brand-building experience to owners, developers and venues who want food & beverage that actually works.

50 Eggs is a hospitality development and management company. We create original restaurant brands, operate them at the highest level, and consult for partners who want the same quality in their own spaces.

Service

Corporate Dining

Workplace dining shouldn’t feel like a cafeteria. We design and operate food & beverage programs for corporate campuses that turn daily meals into a reason to come to the office.

Our approach: chef-driven menus, rotating seasonal concepts, hospitality-grade service, and spaces designed to feel like a restaurant, not a food court.

  • Menu Development
  • Kitchen Design
  • Staff Training
  • Brand Identity
  • Operations Management

Service

Hospitality Consulting

From concept creation to operational turnarounds, we work with owners, developers and operators who want more from their food & beverage programs.

We’ve built six concepts from scratch, launched restaurants in four cities, and grown a brand that was acquired by a public company. We bring that experience to every engagement.

  • Concept Development
  • Menu Engineering
  • Operational Audits
  • Brand Strategy
  • Launch Support

Service

Sports & Venues

Arenas and entertainment venues deserve better food. We create elevated dining concepts designed for the energy and pace of live events — premium quality, fast execution, big atmosphere.

Our Champions Sport Kitchen concept was born from this belief: fans deserve a destination, not a concession stand.

  • Venue Concept Design
  • Fan Experience Strategy
  • Premium F&B Programs
  • Operations & Staffing
