Portfolio

Our Concepts

Six original restaurant brands, each one built from scratch, each one designed to become part of the city it calls home.

Wakuda

Las Vegas · Singapore

A Japanese-Peruvian culinary experience that brings Nikkei cuisine to the global stage. Wakuda is the vision of legendary chef Tetsuya Wakuda, brought to life by 50 Eggs — a restaurant where precision, artistry and soul meet in every dish.

Chica

Las Vegas

Bold, vibrant Latin cuisine that celebrates the flavors of Mexico, Peru, Brazil and Venezuela under one roof. Chica is a party — colorful, loud, unapologetic — and one of the most distinctive dining experiences on the Las Vegas Strip.

Spring Chicken

Miami · Dallas · Las Vegas

Comfort food done right. Spring Chicken is a rotisserie-driven concept built on the idea that everyone deserves great chicken — free-range, herb-roasted, served with sides that steal the show. Fast-casual energy, chef-driven quality.

Bonano Pizza

Dallas

Artisan pizza with an attitude. Bonano Pizza brings New York-style slices and whole pies to Dallas, made with hand-stretched dough, house-made sauce and premium toppings — a neighborhood pizza joint built for repeat visits.
