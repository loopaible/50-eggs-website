What 25 Years in Restaurants Taught Us About Building a Multi-Concept Hospitality Group

50 Eggs Hospitality Group was founded in 2001 by John Kunkel. Since then we’ve opened dozens of restaurants across multiple states and continents, and built brands as different as modern Japanese fine dining and fast-casual fried chicken. The concepts change. The principles don’t.

Cook from scratch, every time

Our reputation started with Yardbird Southern Table & Bar, which opened in Miami Beach in 2011 and was quickly named a James Beard Foundation semifinalist for Best New Restaurant. The lesson has held ever since: guests can taste the difference between food that’s made in-house and food that’s assembled.

“The concepts change. The principles don’t.”

Partner with chefs who define their category

We don’t force one culinary voice across every brand. Instead we pair operators with chefs who are already masters of their craft — like two-Michelin-starred Tetsuya Wakuda, whose Las Vegas restaurant opened in 2022 — and give them the infrastructure to build something singular.

Design the room and the menu together

A restaurant is one experience. Lighting, layout, music, service style and the plate should all tell the same story. Wakuda draws on the energy of Tokyo’s Shinjuku district; Chica brings a Miami-inspired garden feel to the Las Vegas Strip. Neither feels like a template.

Build systems that protect the guest experience

Scaling hospitality means documenting what great looks like — from prep standards to service steps — without turning it into a checklist guests can feel. That discipline is what lets a concept like Spring Chicken run efficiently at a counter while carrying the DNA of a full-service kitchen.

Invest in culture

Great hospitality is delivered by people, and our approach to culture, training and guest service is as central to the business as the menu. It’s also the expertise we share with other operators through our hospitality consulting work.
