Meet Chef Tetsuya Wakuda

Chef Tetsuya Wakuda has spent decades refining a single idea: that Japanese cuisine, done with total precision, needs very little embellishment to be extraordinary. It’s the philosophy that carried him from Sydney to Singapore, and now, in partnership with 50 Eggs Hospitality Group, to Las Vegas.

“I learned early that the ingredient has to lead,” he says. “My job is to get out of its way as much as possible, and only step in where technique actually adds something.” That restraint shows up across the Wakuda menu, from the sashimi service to the claypot rice finished tableside.

“Consistency of philosophy, not consistency of ingredient.”

Seasonality is non-negotiable. The kitchen adjusts sourcing constantly, chasing the best of what’s available rather than locking into a fixed menu for the sake of consistency. “Consistency of philosophy, not consistency of ingredient,” is how he puts it.

Joining 50 Eggs Hospitality Group gave Chef Wakuda a partner who understood that same instinct for hospitality — building a room, a team and a service style around the food rather than the other way around.

Today, that partnership continues to grow, bringing modern Japanese cuisine refined across three continents to a new audience in Las Vegas, with the same uncompromising standard that built his reputation in the first place.
