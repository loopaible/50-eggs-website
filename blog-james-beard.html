50 Eggs Named A James Beard Award Semifinalist

It started with a semifinalist nod for Yardbird back in 2012 — the recognition that first put 50 Eggs Hospitality Group on the national map. More than a decade later, the James Beard Foundation is taking notice again.

The James Beard Awards remain the most closely watched recognition in American restaurants, with semifinalist lists drawn from thousands of nominations across the country. Being named among them once is significant. Being named again, more than ten years and four concepts later, says something about consistency.

“Awards come and go, but the reason we keep showing up is the same reason we started.”

That consistency is the point. Wakuda, Chica, Spring Chicken and Bonanno Brothers were each built differently, for different rooms and different guests, but every one of them was built on the same standard: chef-driven food, served without pretense, by people who care whether you had a good time.

“Awards come and go, but the reason we keep showing up is the same reason we started,” says founder John Kunkel. “Good food, real hospitality, and a team that cares.”

The nod is a reminder that hospitality, done consistently and well over two decades, still gets noticed — and it’s motivation to keep building toward the next one.
