Spring Chicken Launches A New Catering Menu

Spring Chicken’s fried chicken has always traveled well — now there’s a menu built specifically to get it to your table, however many people are at it.

The new catering program keeps the same recipes that come out of the Spring Chicken kitchen every day — the buttermilk-brined fried chicken, the scratch biscuits, the loaded cobb bowls — and scales them for platters, boxed lunches and full spreads.

“Yardbird’s fried chicken, without the wait, wherever you happen to be hosting.”

Ordering is built around simplicity: pick a format, choose your sides, and the team handles the rest, from packaging to delivery timing. Platters are designed to hold their crunch, and sides like mac and cheese and crispy wedges travel in insulated containers built to arrive hot.

It’s the same philosophy that built Spring Chicken in the first place — Yardbird’s fried chicken, without the wait, wherever you happen to be hosting.

Whether it’s a backyard birthday, an office lunch or a full-blown celebration, the new catering menu means Spring Chicken can show up prepared for all of it.
