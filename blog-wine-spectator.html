Wakuda Earns Wine Spectator’s Best of Award of Excellence

Wakuda has been honored with Wine Spectator’s Best of Award of Excellence, placing the restaurant’s wine program among the most respected in the world. It’s a recognition built one bottle, one pairing and one late-night list revision at a time.

The Award of Excellence has been Wine Spectator’s benchmark for outstanding restaurant wine lists for more than four decades. Recipients are chosen for depth, breadth and thoughtfulness — not simply the size of the cellar, but how intelligently it’s built around the food it’s meant to accompany.

“A wine list should read like a menu of its own — every bottle on it earns its place next to the food.”

At Wakuda, that means a list that moves fluidly between Japan and the rest of the world: small-production sake and Japanese whisky sit alongside Burgundy, the Rhône and a growing selection from Australia, a nod to the restaurant’s own Sydney-to-Singapore lineage.

“A wine list should read like a menu of its own,” says the restaurant’s beverage team. “Every bottle on it earns its place next to the food.”

The award joins a growing list of recognitions for 50 Eggs Hospitality Group, and it’s one more reason to linger a little longer over dinner at Wakuda — there’s always one more glass worth trying.
